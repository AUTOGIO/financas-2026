02 · Strategic Problem

The Cost of Financial Fragmentation

◉ Fragmented Visibility
Four banks. Multiple cards. International remittances. No single view of net position. Decisions made without the full picture.
◉ Manual Reconciliation at Scale
Each month required manually cross-referencing PDFs, screenshots, and bank exports. Time-intensive and error-prone with no audit trail.
◉ Subscription Leakage
Recurring charges across multiple platforms accumulated without centralized review. No structured process to evaluate, renew, or cancel services.
◉ Weak Weekly Control Loop
Without a weekly financial review structure, spending signals arrived late — often weeks after the fact, when corrective action was no longer practical.
Board question to consider: How long did it take, historically, to identify an unexpected R$10K+ movement across accounts? The dashboard is designed to reduce that latency to under one week.

07 · Governance & Operating Model

The Weekly Ritual That Keeps This Operational

Operating Cadence
📅
Sunday Update Cycle
Each Sunday, transaction data from all four institutions is manually reviewed and entered into the dashboard. This creates a weekly financial checkpoint with a consistent rhythm.
🔍
Source Document Review
PDFs, bank exports, and card statements from Mercado Pago, Banco Inter, BB Brasil, and BCB Registrato are the primary inputs. Each is reviewed and reconciled against the dashboard.
✅
Cancel Tracker & Subscription Review
Each cycle includes a pass over active subscriptions and the Cancel Tracker. Items are flagged, actioned, or carried forward — preventing passive accumulation of unreviewed recurring spend.
📊
Weekly Snapshot — Auditable Output
Each update produces a current, dated dashboard state. This is the audit artifact for the week — reproducible and reviewable at any future point.
Why Manual — Deliberately
Automation is not the goal at this stage. Manual operation builds the evidence base needed to decide whether automation is justified. Understand before encoding.
Maintainability
The dashboard requires no infrastructure maintenance. Updates are data edits to the HTML file. There is no server to patch, no API to renew, no subscription to manage.
Board question: Is the Sunday ritual being maintained consistently? Completion rate across the first 6-month cycle is the primary leading indicator of Phase 2 readiness.
08 · Risk & Control View

What the Dashboard Controls — and What Remains Open

Data Fragmentation
Four institutions previously siloed. Dashboard unifies all sources into one view with consistent structure.
● Controlled
Recurring Expense Drift
Subscription module and Cancel Tracker create active review loop. 11 items currently in managed queue.
● Controlled
Transfer Visibility
Cash Bridge documents R$122K in inter-account movements previously unexplained. Transfer trail is now auditable.
● Controlled
Bank & Account Exposure
Banks tab provides consolidated view of balances and exposure across all four institutions. BCB Registrato data included.
● Controlled
Manual-Update Bottleneck
Dashboard is only as current as the last Sunday update. If cycle is missed, data lag accumulates. Single point of operational dependency.
⚠ Residual — Monitored
Spend-to-Visibility Timing Gap
Transactions may not appear in the dashboard until the following Sunday. Up to 7 days of spending remain invisible between cycles.
⚠ Residual — Accepted
Controls in place: Data fragmentation, subscription drift, transfer gaps, and account exposure are all addressed by Phase 1. These are material improvements over the prior operating state.
Residual risks: Manual update cadence and 7-day visibility lag are known and accepted. These are the primary drivers of the Phase 2 evaluation criteria.

✓ Zero additional cost
✓ Builds the evidence base
✓ Lowest complexity risk

✗ Gate criteria not yet met

Not recommended at this time

11 · Closing Summary

Build Less. Operate More. Decide with Evidence.

financas-2026 is not a prototype. It is a working financial control layer that is operational today — covering six months, four institutions, 145 transactions, and a R$122K reconciliation. It runs in Safari with no server and no subscription. It is updated weekly by a human who is accountable to a cadence.

The board's mandate is simple: operate it, measure it, and only then scale it. Phase 2 will earn its approval through evidence — not through engineering enthusiasm or architectural ambition.
